02 / When to engage

Bring Synoro in when the decision has outgrown the slide deck.

AI investment

The pilot works. The operating case does not yet exist.

Value has been demonstrated, but production ownership, controls, integration, and recurring cost remain unresolved.

Cyber programme

The backlog is large. The investment priority is unclear.

Technical findings exist, but they do not show which exposures materially affect the business or what should be funded first.

Platform portfolio

The estate has grown faster than its operating model.

Overlapping platforms, inconsistent standards, and divided ownership are increasing cost and slowing delivery.

Transaction

The commercial case depends on technical claims.

An acquisition or partnership requires an independent view of product maturity, scalability, security, and delivery capacity.
